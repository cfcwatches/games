How to play Hue Snake

Guide the snake around the board to eat the glowing dots. Every dot makes the snake longer and scores 10 points. The game ends if you hit a wall or run into your own tail. Steer with the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard, by swiping on the board, or with the on-screen arrows on a phone. Press the space bar or the Pause button to take a break.

Speed and walls

Pick Slow, Normal or Fast. Fast is quick from the start, and every game speeds up a little as your score climbs. Choose Solid walls for the classic game, or Wrap-around to pass straight through one edge and out of the opposite one. Your best score is saved separately for each combination.

Tips

  • Keep to the edges early on and leave the middle free to turn around in.
  • Plan your turn one move ahead. You can queue two quick turns, so a fast U-turn is possible.
  • As the snake grows, move in long, tidy loops rather than zig-zagging.
  • In wrap-around mode, remember that the edges are not safe zones. You can come out of the other side right next to your tail.
