How to play Anagram Scramble

You get a jumble of letters and 90 seconds. Rearrange them into a real word by tapping the letters (or just typing them). When the last slot is filled the word is checked. Get it right and you score points and move straight on to the next one.

Scoring

Each word is worth 10 points per letter. Solve one in under six seconds for a speed bonus, and chain words together without skipping or guessing wrong to build a combo bonus. Skipping costs three seconds, so only skip when you are properly stuck.

Modes

Daily gives everyone the same six-letter words in the same order, and it changes at midnight. Easy, Medium and Hard use five, six and seven letters with a fresh random set every game. Some jumbles can make more than one word, and any real word we recognise is accepted.

Tips

  • Look for common endings first: ING, ED, ER, TION and LY.
  • Pull out letter pairs like TH, CH, SH and QU, and keep them together.
  • Type letters on a keyboard for the fastest play, and press the space bar to reshuffle.
  • Do not stare. If nothing clicks in a few seconds, shuffle to see the letters in a new order.
