Daily and practice modes

Daily mode gives everyone the same word, and it changes at midnight your time. Your streak and win record are saved on this device. Practice mode lets you play as many random words as you like and does not affect your stats.

Hard mode and yesterday’s word

Switch on Hard mode before your first guess and every hint you reveal must be used: green letters stay in place, and letters in the wrong place must appear in your next guess. From day two, the Yesterday button lets you play the previous day’s word if you missed it. It does not count towards your streak.

Tips for a good start

  • Open with a word that uses common letters and different vowels, like STARE, ALONE or HOUSE.
  • Use your second guess to test new letters rather than repeating ones you already know.
  • Watch the keyboard: it remembers every letter you have ruled in or out.
  • Think about common endings and pairs, such as -ING, -ER, TH, SH and CH.
