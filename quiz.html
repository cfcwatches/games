About the quiz

Each round has ten questions drawn at random from five categories: science, history, sport, everyday knowledge and maths. Choose one category to focus on, or leave it on Mixed. You score 10 points for each correct answer, and the timer ends the question after fifteen seconds. Press 1 to 4 on a keyboard to answer quickly.

We check our questions carefully, but mistakes can slip through. If you spot a wrong answer, please get in touch. Fancy a different challenge? Try Maths Sprint or a Sudoku puzzle.
