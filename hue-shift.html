How to play Hue Shift

The board starts as a scrambled version of a smooth colour gradient. Tap a tile, then tap another tile to swap them. Put every tile back in its right place to rebuild the gradient. Tiles marked with a white dot are already correct and cannot be moved, so use them as anchors.

You can try to finish in as few moves as possible, or as fast as possible. Your best result is saved for each size. Hints and the Check button are there if you get stuck, but a game with hints does not set a best score.

Modes

Daily gives everyone the same puzzle, and it changes at midnight. Finishing it counts towards your daily streak on the homepage. Easy, Medium and Hard give you a fresh random puzzle every time, with a bigger grid at each step.

Tips

  • Start with the four corners and work along the edges. The locked tiles tell you the colours at the ends of each row and column.
  • Look for the lightest and darkest tiles first. They sit in different corners and are easy to place.
  • Fill one row at a time. Neighbouring tiles should look like small steps along the same colour line.
  • Use Check to see which tiles are already right, so you do not undo them by mistake.
