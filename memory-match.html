How to play

Pick a size and a theme, then press Start. Tap a card to turn it over, then tap a second card. If they match they stay face up. If not, they flip back, so remember where they were. Clear the board in as few moves as you can: each pair of flips counts as one move.

Why memory games are good for you

Matching games exercise short-term visual memory and attention. The trick is to build a mental map: note the position of every card you flip, even the ones that do not match, and use them on later turns.

  • Start in a corner and work across the board, so you have a system for the cards you have not seen yet.
  • When you flip a card you have already seen, go straight to its partner.
  • Group cards by position in your head, such as top row, left side, centre.
